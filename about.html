The Al-Ameen Heritage

Centuries of Arabian & Indian Perfumery Alchemy, refined for modern connoisseurs and boutique partners worldwide.

The Roots of Pure Oil

Distilling the Soul of Assam Agarwood

Founded on a singular passion for pure olfactory excellence, Al-Ameen Perfumes preserves the sacred art of traditional attar-making. We never dilute with synthetic fillers or carrier alcohols.

Our flagship Al-Ameen Sultan Attar is extracted from wild Assam agarwood, aged in dark oak casks for months to allow its resinous depth to bloom with spicy Kashmiri saffron and dewy Taif roses.

East Meets West

French Precision Meets Oriental Mystique

While our attars embody pure oriental richness, our Eau de Parfum creations bridge Grasse's refined French perfumery standards with Arabian warmth.

Each blend undergoes 14 weeks of cold maceration, resulting in seamless harmony between sparkling top notes and intoxicating, long-lingering base resins.

Olfactory Mastery

The Three Movements of Scent

A master perfume unfolds in a three-stage symphonic journey over hours.

1. Top Notes

The exhilarating initial burst lasting 15 to 30 minutes. Calabrian bergamot, Kashmiri saffron, and pink pepper ignite the senses.

2. Heart Notes

The core emotional character blooming over 2 to 4 hours. Taif rose petals, smoky amber, and French clary sage create an unforgettable aura.

3. Base Notes

The profound anchor clinging to skin and garments for 24+ hours. Aged Assam agarwood, Mysore sandalwood, and ambergris crystals.
