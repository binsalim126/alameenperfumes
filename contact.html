Frequently Asked Questions

How do Wholesale bulk flacon orders work? +
Wholesale orders feature our bulk flacon formulations in 50ml, 100ml, and 500ml sizes directly at wholesale trade rates. You can order single or multiple bulk flacons with direct WhatsApp order confirmation.
Are Al-Ameen attars 100% alcohol-free? +
Yes! All our concentrated attars (such as Al-Ameen Sultan Attar and Zafraan & Sandalwood) are 100% alcohol-free pure oils crafted via slow hydro-distillation.
How fast does pan-India dispatch take? +
Orders placed via WhatsApp are dispatched within 24–48 hours using express air cargo (Blue Dart, DTDC, Delhivery). Delivery typically takes 2–4 business days across India.
Can boutiques order sample vials before placing bulk orders? +
Certainly! We offer our Royal Miniature Attar Set (6 × 6ml) and discovery vials specifically so boutique partners can evaluate our formulations before committing to commercial volumes.
